## 2. In short

- You need **no account** and enter **no personal data**.
- Your **progress and settings stay on your device**. We (the developer) have no server of our own and
  do not receive this data.
- The app shows **only optional video ads** (when you tap an ad button yourself). It uses
  **Google AdMob** for this. Google processes data in the process (see section 4).
- In the EU, the EEA, Switzerland and the UK the app asks for your **consent** to ads at start-up
  (Google "User Messaging Platform"). You can change it at any time in the settings under
  "Ad privacy choices".

## 3. Data on your device

The app stores on your device: game progress (e.g. taler, equipment, collection book, map, arch of
lights), settings (sound, music, vibration, language) and the time of your last daily mine. This data
does not leave the device. You can remove it at any time with "Delete progress" in the settings;
uninstalling the app deletes it as well.

The app uses no analytics or tracking tools of its own, does not ask for your location, contacts,
photos or files, and offers no purchases.

If you copy your daily-mine result, only a short text goes to your device's clipboard – the app does
not send it anywhere.

## 4. Advertising with Google AdMob

**Provider:** Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google").

**What happens:** when the app loads or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically
processes, according to Google:

- your **IP address** (which may be used to estimate an approximate location, e.g. the country),
- **interactions with the app and the ads** (e.g. app launches, taps, video views),
- **diagnostic data** (e.g. launch time, hang rate, energy use),
- **device identifiers**, in particular the Android **advertising ID** and the app set ID.

**Why:** to serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud and abuse. With your consent
Google may also personalise ads; without consent Google shows – where the rules allow –
non-personalised or limited ads, or none at all.

**Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and, for access to your device, the national rules
implementing the ePrivacy Directive) for personalised ads and the storage and access this needs;
where Google processes data for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security, our legitimate
interest in funding the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Consent (Google User Messaging Platform):** before the app requests an ad for the first time it asks
– where required – for your consent in a dialog provided by Google. Until there is an answer that
allows ads, the app does not load any ads. You can review, change or withdraw your choice at any time
in the app's settings via **"Ad privacy choices"**. Withdrawal applies to the future.

**Transfers outside the EU:** Google may also process data in the USA. Google is certified under the
EU-US Data Privacy Framework and also uses standard contractual clauses.

**Retention:** Google decides how long it keeps the data. More information:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

The ad videos are always optional. The game is fully playable without them; offline or without
consent the ad buttons are simply hidden.

## 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17),
restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21) and to withdraw
consent (Art. 7(3)). As we do not store any data about you ourselves, these rights mainly concern the
ad data held by Google; please contact Google (link above) or us at bw.appstudio@gmail.com – we will help.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. in
your place of residence or the authority responsible for us: Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner (Sächsische Datenschutz- und Transparenzbeauftragte), Postfach 11 01 32, 01330 Dresden, Germany, www.datenschutz.sachsen.de.
